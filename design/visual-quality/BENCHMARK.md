# Visual workflow benchmark

Use these three cases when changing the skills. Keep gameplay fixture, asset generation, viewport, text size and time/motion conditions fixed. Preserve the existing result and compare the new result without persuasive captions. A baseline is evidence of current behavior, not automatic artistic approval.

| Case                                  | Realistic request                                                                    | Evidence and success criteria                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Settings: text and controls           | Make Display settings clearer and remove redundant text without changing preferences | [Current frame](references/settings-1920.png); existing labels, options, selected values and keyboard behavior retained; identify repeated section meaning before adding subtitles; stable tab geometry                                               |
| Merchant: artwork and choice          | Make the Boon Merchant easier to compare, with restrained controls and concise copy  | [A](references/merchant-before-1280.png) / [B](references/merchant-candidate-1280.png); exact effects available before purchase; limit/duration/wallet clear; art ratios/profiles and hit areas retained; purchase, reload and leaving remain correct |
| Battle: dense interactive composition | Improve targeting clarity without adding permanent instructions or obscuring cards   | [Current idle frame](references/battle-1920.png); inspect an actual selected-action state before changing anything; preserve row order, seven-card hand reachability, Mana, Swap, End turn, targeting forecast and cancellation                       |

For each run, record concrete findings and fixes for composition, unnecessary copy, artwork cohesion and interaction. Count avoidable instructional/repeated phrases separately from essential gameplay rules. Do not optimize total word count by hiding useful information. Record fit and control reachability at 1280×720 with 125% text, plus the default-size comparison at 1920×1080. Present matching A/B frames for user preference; do not assign the agent's own aesthetic score.

## Initial exercise

The [Merchant pilot](PILOT.md) is the first before/after application. It consolidates two separate context rows, removes the redundant received heading, makes all three effects visible, and reduces competing filled actions. More useful rule text is visible than before; total words are therefore not the success metric. Functional and rendered observations are recorded separately from user preference.

Settings and Battle were captured and visually inspected as transfer cases, without redesigning their production screens. Settings repeats the active Display category as a section legend; the design skill should recognize that repetition instead of adding help copy. Battle already prioritizes illustrations and End turn; the critique skill must inspect targeting before recommending another idle label. These observations exercise routing and restraint, not proof that the skills improve those two screens. Their implementation comparisons remain future benchmark runs, outside this one-screen pilot.

The skills' frontmatter validation checks discoverability and structure only. The initial Merchant result establishes that the workflow can produce a reviewable change; repeat cases and explicit user preference are needed to establish sustained aesthetic improvement.
