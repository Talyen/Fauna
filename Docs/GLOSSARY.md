# Gameplay glossary

Keyword definitions live in [src/game/keywords.ts](../src/game/keywords.ts); the complete gameplay glossary is assembled in [src/game/glossary.ts](../src/game/glossary.ts) and is available in-game under **Menu → Glossary**. Keep terminology in card text and feedback consistent with those definitions.

Terms currently defined: Health, Block, Mana, Action card, Attack, Damage, Hit, Turn, Battle, Draw / discard, Knockout, Keyword XP, Talents, Reserves, Starting lineup, Boons, Currency, Healing, Thorns, Death's Door, defenses, periodic statuses, crowd control, removal effects and each damage type.

Damage, status, defense and keyword contracts are defined in [Keywords](KEYWORDS.md). There are no elemental reactions. Gold is permanent battle-earned currency spent at Merchants, rather than a keyword or talent category. Wish, Consume, Companion and Archery are excluded.

See [Keywords](KEYWORDS.md) for the Alchemy-derived catalog, color identity, current rules, and planned mechanics.

Boon replaces the old relic label for passive journey items. Fire/Burn, Ice/Freeze and Lightning/Stun share talent categories; Thorns, Cleanse and Purge remain combat vocabulary but use Nature, Healing and Light progression respectively. Forge is retired.
