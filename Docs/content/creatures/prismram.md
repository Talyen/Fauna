# Prismram

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Prismram is a 30 Health crystal defender found in ordinary combat packs and collected through recruitment. Its abilities:

- **Prism Charge:** Deal 6 Physical damage
- **Faceted Ward:** Gain 3 Block; Reflect the next harmful status effect
- **Spectrum Lance:** Deal 4 Light damage; Purge target: remove 1 effect

**Refraction** gives harmful status effects received a 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% chance to redirect to a random living enemy at zero through five stars. Faceted Ward grants one guaranteed [Reflect charge](../../KEYWORDS.md#reflection) ahead of Refraction's passive chance, including against Blind.
