# Rookhaven

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Rookhaven is the keeper of forgotten stories, with 26 Health. It joins ordinary encounter packs, recruitment and the starter pool. All three abilities cost 1 Mana:

- **Borrowed Brilliance:** Deal 1 Light damage to all enemies; Blind all enemies
- **Unwritten Ending:** Purge all enemies
- **Margin of Possibility:** Draw 2 cards

**Well Read** gives every card draw a 20%, 35%, 50%, 70%, 85% or 100% chance to create a copy at ranks zero through five. The final tier reads “Drawing a card copies it”. Only living, deployed Rookhavens supply the passive; each supplies its own independent chance, including while controlled. Opening hands, turn draws, ability draws and keyword draws all count. Empty draws, discard recovery, knockout replacements, creating copies and promoting overflow are not draws. Ordinary enemies, including enemy Rookhaven, have no draw pile or hand and receive no alternate passive benefit.

Copies retain the drawn card's owner, definition, level and ordinary Mana cost. They receive distinct instance IDs and enter the hand immediately after the drawn card, using the ordinary FIFO overflow queue when full. Copies remain in the deck for the entire battle, including after play, end-turn discard, recovery and reshuffling. Drawing a previously copied card can copy it again; inserting the new copy never recursively triggers the passive. Each successful copy emits committed action feedback and its rules identify it as a battle copy. Normal card eligibility, XP and control rules apply.

Copies and the next copy ID survive current-format save/resume and deterministic previews. Knockout replacements between ordinary creatures preserve their slots while converting their owners and corresponding definitions to the incoming kit, just like the original cards. Card-driven swaps between ordinary creatures shuffle all departing slots, including copies, back into the draw pile with their incoming definitions. Legendary formation changes remove copies owned by departing creatures while retaining copies of creatures that remain deployed. Victory, defeat and abandonment remove copies before publishing the next phase; the permanent three-card kits remain intact. An extreme battle is bounded to 256 total card instances; reaching that bound stops further copies without dropping original draws or advancing passive RNG. Save validation accepts battle copies only during combat, validates ownership and distinct zone membership, and still requires one permanent instance of each ordinary deployed creature’s three definitions, or two of each for a Legendary. Save versioning follows the [shared contract](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#save-contract).

Borrowed Brilliance uses the shared area-attack rules, including Dodge, Critical hits, Block, retaliation and room scaling. Its subsequent Blind applies once to every living opposing creature through the existing harmful-status owner, including Nullify and Reflect interactions. Retaliation that knocks out Rookhaven stops its remaining hits and Blind. Unwritten Ending removes all beneficial effects, including Block, Armor, both Thorns types, Dodge, Reflect and Nullify variants, from each living opponent. It retains harmful effects and uses the ordinary Armor-depletion reactions; those reactions can stop a defeated caster. It needs no chosen target. Its complete Purge is unchanged by card level or room power. Auto-play uses it when a living enemy has a removable beneficial effect. Margin of Possibility draws through the shared draw owner, triggering Well Read separately for each actual draw. Enemies use Borrowed Brilliance and Unwritten Ending against the deployed player team, and exclude the unsupported draw ability.

## Artwork provenance and validation record

The unchanged creature portrait (`Rookhaven_03.png`) and the retained Borrowed Brilliance, Unwritten Ending and Margin of Possibility action illustrations come from the user's [Rookhaven Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16A7CWiutkxd-XLHU805kMGUYCZ3QfcTX). The manifest records each source URL and rights note. Original PNGs are preserved under `public/art/rookhaven/`; preparation retains the landscape and portrait compositions without cropping. The four source images were inspected together at card-like scale. Focused Chromium review covered the Collection kit, all six rank pages, a full seven-card battle hand, the copied-card tooltip, selection and execution of all three abilities, and copy persistence after reload at 1920×1080 with default text and 1280×720 with 125% text. The source set retained readable silhouettes and uncropped compositions at those sizes; rules, target feedback and controls fit without scrolling. Review caught and corrected missing all-enemy UI targeting for Unwritten Ending, with a keyboard confirmation regression added to the targeting suite. The task preview used isolated dependency caches after an outdated Vite optimization cache prevented its initial load. Screenshots and geometry/error metadata are preserved in task reports. This is browser evidence with reduced motion; full CI, native platforms, physical controllers and balance acceptance remain pending.
