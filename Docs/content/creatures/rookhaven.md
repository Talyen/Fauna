# Rookhaven

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/rookhaven.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Rookhaven is the keeper of forgotten stories. It joins ordinary encounter packs, recruitment and the starter pool.

**Well Read** gives every card draw a 20%, 35%, 50%, 70%, 85% or 100% chance to create a copy at ranks zero through five. The final tier reads “Drawing a card copies it”. Only living, deployed Rookhavens supply the passive; each supplies its own independent chance, including while controlled. Opening hands, turn draws, ability draws, keyword draws and direct Discard recovery all count under the shared [draw rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#draws-and-returns). Empty draws, card returns from Dream Yo-Yo or Cloud Cup-and-Ball, knockout replacements, creating copies and promoting overflow are not draws. Ordinary enemies, including enemy Rookhaven, have no draw pile or hand and receive no alternate passive benefit.

Copies retain the drawn card's owner, definition, level and ordinary Mana cost. They receive distinct instance IDs and enter the hand immediately after the drawn card, using the ordinary FIFO overflow queue when full. Copies remain in the deck while their owner stays deployed, including after play, end-turn discard, recovery and reshuffling. Drawing a previously copied card can copy it again; inserting the new copy never recursively triggers the passive. Each successful copy emits committed action feedback and its rules identify it as a battle copy. Normal card eligibility, XP and control rules apply.

Copies and the next copy ID survive current-format save/resume and deterministic previews. Replacing an ordinary creature removes its battle copies from every zone; permanent slots retain their IDs and convert to the incoming owner and kit. Card-driven swaps shuffle those permanent slots into the draw pile. Legendary formation changes remove copies owned by departing creatures while retaining copies of creatures that remain deployed. Victory, defeat and abandonment remove copies before publishing the next phase; the permanent three-card kits remain intact. An extreme battle is bounded to 256 total card instances; reaching that bound stops further copies without dropping original draws or advancing passive RNG. Save validation accepts battle copies only during combat, validates ownership and distinct zone membership, and still requires one permanent instance of each ordinary deployed creature’s three definitions, or two of each for a Legendary. Save versioning follows the [shared contract](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#save-contract).

Borrowed Brilliance uses the shared area-attack rules, including Dodge, Critical hits, Block, retaliation and room scaling. Its subsequent Blind applies once to every living opposing creature through the existing harmful-status owner, including Nullify and Reflect interactions. Retaliation that knocks out Rookhaven stops its remaining hits and Blind. Unwritten Ending removes all beneficial effects, including Block, Armor, Thorns, Dodge, Reflect and Nullify variants, from each living opponent. It retains harmful effects and uses the ordinary Armor-depletion reactions; those reactions can stop a defeated caster. It needs no chosen target. Its complete Purge is unchanged by card level or room power. Auto-play uses it when a living enemy has a removable beneficial effect. Margin of Possibility draws through the shared draw owner, triggering Well Read separately for each actual draw. Enemies use Borrowed Brilliance and Unwritten Ending against the deployed player team, and exclude the unsupported draw ability.
