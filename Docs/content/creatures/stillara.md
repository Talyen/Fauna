# Stillara

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/stillara.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Stillara is available through ordinary encounters, recruitment and later starter drafts. Its support-only kit excludes it from the first solo starter pool.

**First Thaw** reduces Freeze damage taken by living deployed allies, including Stillara, by 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% at zero through five stars. It applies before rounding and Block, and multiplies with other resistances. Multiple Stillara use the strongest active tier. Backups and knockouts neither supply nor receive protection; enemies protect their own side.

Thaw Path removes all accumulated Freeze from living deployed allies, including Stillara, without removing active Freeze, Stun, immunity or other harmful effects. Actual removal triggers ordinary Cleanse rewards once per recipient; an empty removal grants none. Water Remembers heals each living deployed ally, including Stillara, then draws once even if nobody needed healing. Neither card affects backups or revives knockouts. Reed Reservoir grants its Mana after paying its cost and before automatic end turn, respecting the shared Mana cap. Enemies rank Thaw Path and Water Remembers on their own side through the shared [decision policy](../../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#enemy-decisions), with authored healing and explicit room power and no player-only draws or Mana grants.
