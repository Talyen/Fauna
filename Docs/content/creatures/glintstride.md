# Glintstride

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/glintstride.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Glintstride combines Light damage with Blind and Dodge.

**Wrong Foot** reads “Glintstride’s attacks deal 1 additional Light damage to Blinded enemies” at zero stars, scaling to 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 at one through five stars. Each landed attack packet against an already Blinded enemy adds one supplemental Light packet through ordinary defenses and Light interactions. Misses, periodic damage, retaliation, derived damage and supplemental procs do not trigger another bonus. Other creatures do not receive this passive. Blind remains until its owner's next attack; False Horizon uses ordinary Nullify and Reflect handling without dealing damage. Enemy Glintstride uses its own side's targeting. Mirage Run's draw applies only to players.
