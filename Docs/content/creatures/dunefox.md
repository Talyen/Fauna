# Dunefox

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/dunefox.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Dunefox is an evasive support creature available through ordinary encounters, recruitment and starter drafts.

**Shifting Sands** shares part of Dunefox’s Dodge chance with allies. Other living deployed allies receive that fraction of Dunefox's intrinsic current Dodge chance, including the ordinary 5%, talents and temporary bonuses, before adding received Shifting Sands bonuses. Multiple providers contribute independently without recursive amplification; Dunefox does not benefit from its own aura. Total Dodge remains capped at 75%. Knockouts and backup providers contribute nothing; swaps, control and expiring bonuses update the live aura automatically.

Dune Whirl applies [Blind](../../KEYWORDS.md#blind) to surviving enemies after damage, including those that Dodged it.

Mirage Step uses the shared [temporary Dodge rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#temporary-dodge). Enemies retain its 30% Dodge without the player-only draw.
