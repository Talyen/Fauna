# Emberback

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/emberback.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Emberback is an armored brawler. Molten Shell grants no starting Armor.

Molten Shell triggers once per positive-to-zero Armor transition, after the effect that removes Armor completes. Damage, periodic damage, shattering, Purge and Armor theft can break Armor. No burst occurs while Armor remains, when Armor was already zero, or after the creature is knocked out. New Armor can enable another burst. Burn packets use existing mitigation and buildup, without Dodge, Critical hits or ordinary attack retaliation. Player bursts use the collected tier and hit living enemies; enemy bursts use the base tier and hit deployed living allies.
