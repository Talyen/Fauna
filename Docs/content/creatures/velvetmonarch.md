# Velvetmonarch

Legendary · The garden sovereign

[Authored kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/velvetmonarch.ts) · [Drive artwork](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1mzW8TvkU39cDslXpw46q-vdJX-gZnEg5) · [Legendary rules](../../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#legendaries-and-minions)

Velvetmonarch enters with two Royal Blooms, each with 6 Health. When Velvetmonarch actually gains Block, each living, uncontrolled Bloom attacks an independently selected random living opponent for Nature damage. The player passive scales from 1 to 6 damage across six tiers; enemies use the base tier. The Block grant resolves first. A capped or fully converted grant does not trigger, and giving Block to a Bloom alone does not trigger. Blooms use ordinary attack, Dodge, Blind, retaliation and reflection rules. Resolve Blooms in slot order and stop if their owner falls or no opponents remain.

Royal Repose restores Velvetmonarch's Health, then grants Block to each living deployed ally, including Blooms. Meadow Decree summons one Bloom in the first empty position, then restores Health to all living allies; with both positions full it still heals. Summoned Bloom Health gains 2 per ability upgrade level. Sovereign's Wake attacks every enemy with Nature damage, then applies Blind under the shared attack and status rules. Repeats resolve their effects normally, including each actual Block grant and Bloom response.

Blooms have no autonomous turns or cards and follow the shared Minion lifecycle. Defeating one stops its response immediately; they leave when their owner falls or departs and return on entry. Shared group healing includes living backup only when Camp Hammock applies. The boss and recruited creature use the same ability definitions.

The portrait and three selected action artworks are newer Drive variants. On 2026-10-29, the user selected the crowned-cup Royal Bloom artwork from three fresh built-in ImageGen options based on the [Fauna locked art-style prompt](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VODYgwwZO9leeR5xLgc5OoVdbefRiYRv/view). The original selection and manifest retain the artwork and provenance; this choice covers the Bloom artwork, not the rest of the battle screen. Balance and hosted CI remain separate qualification steps.
