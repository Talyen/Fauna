# Bloomquail

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/bloomquail.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Bloomquail is a support creature available through ordinary encounters, recruitment and later three-creature starter drafts.

**Full Bloom** reads "Convert 50% of Block you gain into Health for the most injured ally" at zero stars, increasing to 60%, 70%, 80% and 90%. Its five-star text is "Convert Block you gain into Health for the most injured ally". Conversion happens immediately whenever deployed Bloomquail gains Block from a card, talent or shared grant. Only the newly gained, stack-capped amount participates; existing Block is untouched. Round the converted amount normally and cap it by the recipient's missing Health, retaining the remainder as Block. Full-Health teams keep all gained Block. Select the living deployed ally with the lowest remaining Health percentage, including Bloomquail; battlefield order breaks ties. Backup Bloomquail cannot convert Block, and knockouts cannot receive healing. Camp Hammock extends the automatic healing pool to living backups under the [shared recipient rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#healing-and-leech). Use the shared [passive-healing rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#repeats-and-derived-effects).

Enemies use the base 50% conversion and heal their own side. Bloom Cradle draws two cards then restores two Health to Bloomquail itself; its healing never targets another ally. Enemies omit its player-only draw and heal themselves through authored healing and explicit room power. Petal Veil heals each living ally and applies Blind to opponents once per play, independently of how many allies were healed. Its Blind remains useful when allies are already at full Health.
