# Chimecricket

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Chimecricket is a 24 Health musical support creature available through starter drafts, ordinary encounters and recruitment. Its abilities:

- **Resonant Leap:** Deal 4 Stun damage; Draw a card
- **Hush Dome:** Nullify the next harmful status effect on each ally
- **Lullaby Drift:** Restore 4 Health and Cleanse the most injured ally

**Encore** gives Chimecricket's first accepted ability each turn a 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% chance to repeat at zero through five stars. Use the shared [repeat rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#repeats-and-derived-effects); talent repeats and Encore share the two-resolution cap. A failed roll spends that turn's opportunity; rejection does not. The receipt survives swaps and current-format saves and resets at the next turn or battle. Lullaby Drift reselects the most injured living deployed ally for each repetition, including Chimecricket, using Health percentage and battlefield order for ties; healing and full Cleanse affect that same recipient. Backup and knockouts are excluded.

Hush Dome gives each living deployed ally, including Chimecricket, one [Nullify protection](../../KEYWORDS.md#nullify). Enemy Hush Dome protects only living enemies.
