# Ringpalm

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Ringpalm has 30 Health and is available through starter drafts, ordinary encounters and recruitment. Its abilities:

- **Ringclap:** Deal 6 Stun damage
- **Harmonic Set:** Deal 1 Stun damage to all enemies; Gain 1 Armor; 20% chance to repeat
- **Seedling Circle:** Grant all allies 2 Block or Thorns

**Resonance** gives Ringpalm's Stun damage a 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% chance to repeat at zero through five stars. Each Stun packet that removes Health rolls independently while both source and target remain alive, including supplemental Stun damage from talents and packets in repeated abilities. Misses, fully mitigated packets, periodic damage, retaliation and derived damage do not roll. The repeat uses the same base packet with ordinary defenses and Stun buildup, without another Dodge, Critical, retaliation, attack-triggered talent chain or Resonance roll.

Harmonic Set independently rolls its 20% chance once per accepted play and repeats the complete ability once, including its Armor. Intrinsic, Encore and talent ability repeats together still allow at most two complete resolutions; each resolution can independently trigger Resonance on each enemy. [Repeat rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#repeats-and-derived-effects) govern payment, XP and actor/target knockout.

Seedling Circle rolls one seeded, equally likely choice per resolution and gives that same protection to every living deployed ally, including Ringpalm. Backups and knockouts are excluded. Block and Thorns use their ordinary grant rules and passive interactions. Enemies protect only their own side using authored support values with explicit room power. Classification awards Block and Nature XP once per accepted play, matching other cards with random alternative effects. Previews use detached state and preserve the source RNG; current-format saves retain the resulting outcomes.
