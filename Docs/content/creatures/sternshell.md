# Sternshell

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Sternshell has 32 Health and is available through starter drafts, ordinary encounters and recruitment. Its abilities:

- **Surge Ram:** Deal 3 Physical damage; Doubled if you have Armor
- **Siphon Vault:** Swap out for a random backup; Draw 1 card
- **Spiral Return:** Deal 1 Water damage to all enemies; Gain 1 Mana

**Shelter** grants Sternshell 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 Armor at combat start, from zero through five stars. Enemies use the base 2 Armor. It follows ordinary opening grants: deployed creatures receive it once per battle; swaps and resume do not grant it again. Sternshell starting in backup receives no opening Armor.

Surge Ram doubles its intrinsic damage, including card upgrades, while its actor has Armor; ordinary offensive modifiers and target defenses then apply. It does not spend Armor. Enemies use the same doubling condition with explicit room power. Siphon Vault follows [ordinary swap rules](../../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#knockout-replacement-and-ability-swaps) and always draws one, including without backup. Spiral Return refunds Mana once after its attacks, before automatic end turn. Enemy abilities omit player-only draws and Mana refunds.
