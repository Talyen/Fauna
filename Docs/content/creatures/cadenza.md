# Cadenza

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Cadenza has 28 Health and is available through ordinary encounters, recruitment and later three-creature drafts. Its support-only kit excludes it from the first solo starter pool. Its abilities:

- **Resonant Well:** Draw 2 cards; Gain 1 Mana
- **Echoes Remember:** Return a random discarded card to your hand
- **Rousing Refrain:** Give all allies 20% Dodge until your next turn; Draw 1 card

**Living Chorus** restores 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 Health to the most injured living deployed ally after the team's second accepted card each turn. Any ally's card counts, including free plays; rejected commands and repeated resolutions do not count. Healing resolves after the card's effects, once per turn, while Cadenza is alive and deployed, including while controlled. The existing team counter survives swaps and current-format resume and resets each turn and battle. Backup and knocked-out creatures neither supply nor receive the healing. Use the shared [passive-healing rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#repeats-and-derived-effects); enemies trigger after their side's second ability each turn.

Echoes Remember uses seeded randomness over eligible discarded cards, including its own just-played card. Eligibility follows the shared draw owner, including neutral Food and fallen-owner cards when Oathara supplies access. It draws the existing card through the ordinary hand/overflow owner, retaining identity and allowing Well Read and draw-related reactions under the shared [draw rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#draws-and-returns). It does not reshuffle the draw pile and card upgrades do not increase its one-card recovery. Resonant Well draws before refunding Mana, respecting ordinary caps and refunding before automatic end turn. Rousing Refrain grants [temporary Dodge](../../KEYWORDS.md#temporary-dodge) to each living deployed ally; it draws once. Enemies can choose Rousing Refrain through the shared [enemy decision policy](../../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#enemy-decisions), without player-only draw, Mana or discard recovery.
