# Ravelland

[Creature index](README.md) · [Kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/ravelland.ts) · [Regressions](../../../tests/unit/game/creatures/ravelland.test.ts) · [Drive artwork](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1q8s6_iCyWENNsyRUGvIJde-WzZdVs0NB)

Ravelland is a Legendary stone elk carrying a grove across a canyon. Its wide antlers open the cliffs, its hoof turns a rockslide aside, and its antlers divert rain around settlements. The portrait and Antler Pass, Canyon Rebuke and Rainward are unchanged selections; the manifest records exact sources. Pilgrim Causeway, Moving Grove and Faultsense remain upstream alternatives.

## Kit and strategy

Faultline grants 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 Armor when damage breaks Ravelland's Block, from zero through five stars. Armor arrives after the damage that broke Block is resolved, so it cannot mitigate that hit. Partial absorption, Purge, Block decay and a knockout do not trigger it. A surviving deployed Ravelland can trigger it again whenever damage breaks newly acquired Block; normal Armor caps and modifiers apply. Enemy Ravelland uses the zero-star tier.

Antler Pass deals 8 Physical damage to one opponent, Canyon Rebuke deals 3 Physical damage to all opponents, and Rainward gives Ravelland 6 Block. All cost 1 Mana and their primary values gain two per ability level. The higher damage values are provisional solo-Legendary tuning: a six-card deck has no deployed teammates to contribute damage, while Rainward trades tempo for later protection. Room power scales the enemy's cards through the normal rules.

Rainward followed by a broken shield builds a second, lasting layer of protection. The player can break Block with a large hit, use Water to bypass Armor reduction, or Purge protection. Ravelland has no healing and can be worn down when its Block cards are absent. Coralcurl also reacts to broken Block, but retaliates against the attacker immediately; Faultline instead changes the value of later Physical and Stun attacks. Mossguard retains Block and Sternshell begins with Armor, while neither converts a broken shield into new Armor. Armor and Block Talents or Boons can strengthen the loop, but cannot create an extra trigger without breaking newly gained Block.

Ravelland occupies the final Boss encounter and retains the same passive and six-card solo kit when recruited. Its numbers and encounter pacing are initial estimates; neither artistic acceptance nor measured balance follows from source implementation alone.
