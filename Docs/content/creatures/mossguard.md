# Mossguard

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/mossguard.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Mossguard combines self-protection, group Stun damage and group healing. Moss Renewal includes its actor. Group healing cannot revive knockouts; Camp Hammock includes living backups under the [shared recipient rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#healing-and-leech). Upgrades and explicit gameplay modifiers use the existing rules.

Overgrowth increases Block retention relative to the ordinary 50%, with normal rounding. It applies on each turn transition, including in backup. Enemy Mossguard always uses the base tier.
