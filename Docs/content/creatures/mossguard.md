# Mossguard

[Creature index](README.md) · [Current kit](../../../src/game/content/creatures/mossguard.ts) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Mossguard replaces the retired Mossback content. It joins ordinary encounters, recruitment and collected starter drafts, but is absent from the current [first-run starter pool](../../../src/game/content.ts). Its kit combines self-protection, group Stun damage and group healing. Moss Renewal includes its actor. Group healing cannot revive knockouts; Camp Hammock includes living backups under the [shared recipient rules](../../KEYWORDS.md#healing-and-leech). Upgrades and explicit gameplay modifiers use the existing rules.

Overgrowth retains 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% more Block at zero through five stars. This is a relative increase over the ordinary 50% retention: 55%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 75% or 80% of remaining Block survives, rounded normally. It applies on each turn transition, including in backup. Enemy Mossguard always uses the base tier.
