# Mossguard

[Creature index](README.md) · [Authoring contracts](../../CONTENT.md)

Mossguard is a new species ID replacing the removed Mossback content and occupies its former starter and encounter slots. He has 32 Health and three abilities: Boulder Brace grants 6 Block to himself, Rooted Stomp deals 2 Stun damage to every living enemy, and Moss Renewal restores 2 Health to every living deployed ally, including himself. Group healing does not revive knockouts or affect backup. Upgrades and explicit gameplay modifiers use the existing rules.

Overgrowth retains 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50% or 60% more Block at zero through five stars. This is a relative increase over the ordinary 50% retention: 55%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 75% or 80% of remaining Block survives, rounded normally. It applies on each turn transition, including in backup. Enemy Mossguard always uses the base tier.
