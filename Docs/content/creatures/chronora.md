# Chronora

Legendary · The keeper of borrowed moments

[Legendary rules](../../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#legendaries-and-minions) · [Drive artwork](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ugPfCfP_IsQ_4XQFXJKzSqfbC9KHElhu)

Chronora enters battle with two Pendulums. Each has 6 Health and adds 1 damage to each Chronora attack. They have no cards or autonomous turns; damage bonuses do not depend on control. A defeated Pendulum stops contributing immediately. Pendulums disappear on their owner's departure or defeat and return fresh when Chronora enters the field again.

Turn Back the Tide prepares one chance to repeat Chronora's next attack whenever Chronora actually restores Health. The six tiers are 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100 percent. Additional healing does not stack preparations. The chance is rolled and consumed when an attack is played, including a miss; ordinary repeat limits apply. Overhealing and resurrection do not prepare it.

Borrowed Moment gives Negate to all living deployed allies. Pendulum Break deals 4 Stun damage before the living Pendulums' bonuses. Bridge Rewind restores 4 Health to Chronora, then restores one defeated Pendulum in left-to-right order; with both alive, it only restores Health. All cost 1 Mana; primary damage and healing gain 2 per upgrade level.

The portrait and three selected ability images are retained under `public/art/chronora/`. The Pendulum illustration was generated using the portrait as a reference with built-in ImageGen, using a floating gold clock with a dark blue ribbon, simple silhouette and a matte gouache sky.
