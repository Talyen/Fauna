# Orifable Food artwork prompts

Proposed Apple and Mana Berries artwork generated with built-in ImageGen on October 23, 2026. Originals are preserved unchanged under [design/food](../../../design/food/). These sources are not registered runtime assets or approved in-game selections.

## Style provenance

The style clause comes from the owner-provided [Orifable original prompts](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1c0et_eq5s4e5XuVY0QifEYXz1c6Po-4E/view) and [ability utility prompts](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Qa2-GbS5KEeEpnQOihrAQmAnHUdh3QuW/view). The [Care Package illustration](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10F-sy4aL0O3B3YWcF1hp4gqIRtErj3cp/view) supplied the visual reference for palette, setting and painted treatment. It was inspected before generation; these were new text-prompt generations, with no reference image attached to the tool calls.

The asset job is independent Food-card artwork, full-bleed 3:4 portrait, with a readable focal subject at the shared 160–200px short-side card profiles. No creature identity or rules are baked into either image.

Opaque full scene background. Keep the supporting setting quiet, strongly subordinate to the food. Subject and composition: a SINGLE fresh red apple with a curved brown stem and one broad green leaf, large and appealing, filling most of the composition. Resting on a simply folded cream picnic cloth on a sunlit worn stone ledge. Rich vermilion and warm golden-red shapes, one broad cream highlight, a few deliberately simplified painted planes; no photorealistic skin detail or tiny speckles. Background is a few broad muted shapes suggesting a leafy orchard, old honey-colored stone and deep teal shade. The apple's readable silhouette and nourishing freshness are the entire story. No other food, no magical aura, no decorative sparkle.

## Evidence scope

Both originals are 1086 × 1448 pixels. Source review inspected the complete images and 160px/200px short-side thumbnails together. Silhouettes, red-versus-blue color separation, opaque backgrounds and the absence of text were checked. This is source-level review, not prepared-output, ArtCard, battle interaction or artistic acceptance evidence.
