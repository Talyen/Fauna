# Boon contracts

Use the [Boon design skill](../../.agents/skills/fauna-boon-design/SKILL.md) for artwork-led proposals and mechanic redesigns.

[boons.ts](../../src/game/content/boons.ts) owns available Boons, IDs, names and catalog order. [Boon rules](../../src/game/rules/boons.ts) own numeric parameters and their displayed descriptions; execution and visible assessments use those same parameters. [Modifier bindings](../../src/game/modifier-bindings.ts) identify the implementation functions; [Boon regressions](../../tests/unit/game/boons.test.ts) protect timing, caps, actual-outcome triggers and recursion limits.

The original portraits are unchanged WebP selections copied from `Alchemy: src/assets/optimized/`. New portraits use user-provided PNG originals from the Drive Boon collection, prepared through the shared card recipe; the manifest records per-file sources, rights and hashes. Light replaces Alchemy's Holy terminology. Forge, Wish, Potion and Companion Boons are excluded because Fauna lacks their mechanics; Consume-dependent talents remain excluded; the explicitly approved Nectar Dipper affects Food without introducing a general Consume-triggered Boon.

## Saved effects

[The current save format](../../src/game/save-format.json) and [save validation](../../src/game/save.ts) own encoding and validation; [development saves](../../AGENTS.md#development-saves) have no migration path. Status source attribution for Blind and control is saved alongside periodic effects. Rainkeeper's Mana allowance, the shared next-card repeat grant and Encore Baton's cross-turn progress use the existing saved combat owners.

Story Puzzle Cube saves zero through two distinct successful harmful-effect types; new battles clear the set and validation rejects progress without the Boon. Mercury Metronome saves the last acting roster ally; validation rejects unknown actors or progress without ownership. Maximum Mana supports seven crystals plus Rainkeeper's two overflow points. The [October 25 elegant artifact additions](../task-evidence/2026-10-25-elegant-boons.md) add no saved counters or pending resolution. Status-only reflection, including Prismram, retains its ordinary contract.

[Journey rules](../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#currency-and-items) own reward pools and journey-only ownership; [Merchant rules](../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md#optional-support-visits) own stock and prices. Inspect new portraits through the [UI review procedure](../UI.md#required-review). Dated [Boon delivery records](../archive/README.md#boons) retain approved revisions, source identities and verification limits.

## Nine artwork-led Boons

- **Aether Gramophone:** The second resolution of an allied repeated ability cannot be Dodged, including its area recipients and authored damage extras. Blind is consumed for the attack, but its miss cannot defeat the repeat; Negate, attack reflection, Block and Armor retain their normal rules. Reflected attacks do not inherit the protection
- **Herbarium Press:** An enemy Frozen when a Cleanse application begins retains its Poison through that application, even if the same Cleanse removes Freeze. Other harmful effects and allied Poison follow ordinary Cleanse rules
- **Honey Press:** Each actual enemy Block pool removed by allied Purge restores half its removed amount, rounded normally, to the living ally with the lowest Health fraction. Pattern Reel and Winnowing Sieve retain their ordinary linked removals and each actual removal can heal; Camp Hammock extends eligible healing to backups
- **Rook of Mercy:** An ability swap grants 2 Armor to each living allied creature entering the formation. A position exchange grants it to both participants; knockout replacement is not an ability swap. Armor uses ordinary modifiers and gain reactions
- **Tidecrest Hairpin:** Allied Thorns retain at least one stack when retaliating, including Briar Racquet; Purge can still remove them
- **Stardust Whetstone:** A landed allied Physical attack packet against an enemy with Armor at impact applies 1 Bleed through ordinary Nullify and reflection, including a hit absorbed by Block. Damage, Armor wear and the existing hit stay unchanged
- **Ashfall Pendant:** Enemy Burn lost to ordinary decay removes the same amount of remaining Block, capped at that Block. This removal is not attack damage or Purge and does not trigger Block-break rewards; Cinder Fan still uses the same decayed Burn
- **Wishing Building Block:** An actual allied Armor gain grants 1 Block to every other living deployed ally already holding Armor. The recipient uses ordinary Block gain and sharing; capped Armor grants and undeployed providers do not trigger it
- **Sovereign Toast Rack:** Each accepted Food play grants 2 Armor to every living deployed ally, including Minions, after Food effects. Food repeats do not grant additional Armor; the grant uses ordinary Armor modifiers and gain reactions

## Hearth and cadence Boons

These Boons use the ordinary reward pools and their portraits from the user-selected Drive collection. Rules and values remain in the Boon rule owner. Their combat interactions use the following contracts:

- **Quiet Pinwheel:** Living controlled allies retain their ordinary Dodge chance and sharing; enemy control and Blind misses keep their normal rules
- **Lagoon Chalice:** Allied Water ability attacks and authored Water damage extras Leech when their attacker has Block; reflected enemy attacks retain their own rules
- **Acorn Skittle:** A landed allied Stun attack against an already Stunned enemy deals 2 Stun damage to the first other living enemy, with ordinary status defenses; the extra damage cannot chain
- **Wonder Kaleidoscope:** Allied Blind reflection also applies Blind to every other living enemy, respecting Nullify without further reflection
- **Welcome Doormat:** Ability swaps copy an existing Nullify between their living participants without consuming it; replacements do not qualify
- **Shelter Lantern:** Allied Block at the start of Purge protects other effects for that entire Purge application; the Purge can still remove Block
- **Chestnut Roaster:** A landed allied Burn hit that encounters enemy Block applies twice the normal Burn buildup; the original damage is unchanged
- **Chamomile Compress:** Actual allied Health restoration grants Block equal to Bleed at restoration, before a subsequent Cleanse can remove it; overhealing alone does not qualify
- **Cadence Panpipes:** A repeated single-target attack additionally hits the first other living enemy; its target-directed damage and Purge follow the attack, while self-directed and group effects are not duplicated. Area attacks gain no extra target; Dream Tangram can still continue the repeat after a defeat
- **Hearthcap Kettle:** Each accepted Food play Cleanses one effect from the living deployed ally with the lowest Health fraction after Food and its repeat; ordinary Cleanse propagation and rewards apply
