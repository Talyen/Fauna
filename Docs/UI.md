# Fauna design system

Fauna's screens are game surfaces. Show cards, decision-relevant state, and the actions required to play. The background is solid black (`#000`). No promotional copy, decorative chrome, permanent save-success indicators, or visible combat log.

Screen composition is mostly center-aligned: headings, content groups, artwork captions, and primary action rows. Preserve paired numeric alignment, readable rule paragraphs, non-scrolling tabs, and battle controls whose positions convey meaning.

## Shared components and enforcement

Use source-owned shadcn/ui for every standard UI control and its interaction behavior. Current approved primitives: Button, Dialog, AlertDialog, Tooltip, Tabs, Switch, RadioGroup, and Progress. Settings uses Sound and Auto-end turn Switches and a labeled text-size RadioGroup; End journey uses AlertDialog with Cancel initially focused and no outside-click dismissal. Preserve native Tab, arrow-key, Space, Enter, Escape, focus trapping and return behavior. W/A/S/D and gamepad navigation adapt to those components rather than replacing their state machines.

`scripts/shadcn-registry.json` explicitly lists reviewed primitives and their Radix owners. New standard controls must come from upstream shadcn, retain provenance, and be registered during review. Do not build custom toggles, tabs, dropdowns, dialogs, or clickable layout elements. The check rejects raw native controls, handmade widget roles, direct Radix imports, unregistered primitives, competing UI libraries and inline visual styles. These source guards complement interaction tests; they cannot prove all behavior by static analysis.

Explicit gameplay compositions are artwork cards, the hand fan, targeting, mana crystals, combat feedback and forecast overlays on shadcn Progress bars. They use shadcn controls where interactive and shared tokens; no general-purpose custom widget is permitted under this exception. Black canvas, typography, semantic combat colors and minimum hit areas remain project theme decisions.

ArtCard is the sole artwork surface: exactly one image. Battlefield health bars are the sole UI overlay, placed just inside the bottom edge; other text and UI stay outside artwork. Creature artwork stays 4:3 landscape and action artwork 3:4 portrait, with contain rendering; never stretch or crop to another ratio. Names, rules, health numbers and traits belong in hover/focus details, including within centered pinned inspection. Dynamic progress geometry is owned by Progress; hand geometry by FanSlot. All visual colors, text sizes, and radii use shared tokens. The CI static gate (`npm run check:full`) enforces component ownership, palette and typography rules.

Keywords in rule prose are semibold inline words in their semantic colors, with normal word spacing. They have no individual hover tooltip, click action or keyboard focus stop; definitions remain in the Glossary. Rules inside a surrounding hover/inspection tooltip retain this same plain-text treatment.

## Battle composition

- Horizontally centered enemy row above horizontally centered active creatures, with a compact overlapping fan at the bottom. Hand cards match creature artwork-frame height even while rotated, raised, or selected. Uniform compensation preserves each original aspect ratio. Keep the chosen overlap.
- Creature-first scale and spacing follow the reviewed A composition. Selection stays raised in its hand slot. Hover/focus lifts a card; selected cards lift further. The composition must fit every supported viewport and text setting without scrolling.
- Thin 4px health bars sit inside battlefield frames, aligned with the bottom border. Status icons sit below each frame, with horizontal pagination for more than three effects. Numeric health appears only in hover/inspection and accessible descriptions. Block retains its external icon and count. Forecasts show health-bar changes and exact effect amounts without visible HP totals.
- Hide enemy intents and battle, turn, draw and discard counters. Mana uses Alchemy's original full/spent crystals, sockets, fade and glint; provenance is in the battle-layout sample README.
- Acting creatures use a quiet neutral external outline; valid recipients have external corner brackets, with gold brackets on the active recipient. A thin arrow connects the actor to the active recipient outside the artwork. Mouse selection waits for a hovered recipient and clears its forecast on leave; keyboard/controller selection immediately previews the remembered valid recipient. Click the target or press Enter/controller A to commit, even with only one valid recipient. Team-wide actions bracket and preview every living ally and use Play. There is no extra targeted Play button. A compact selected-action summary keeps the action name, final rules and recipient or selection prompt visible.
- A/D switches valid targets, remembering the last valid enemy/ally. W/S moves between rows; A/D moves within a row. I/controller X or right-click opens pinned inspection. Escape/controller B cancels, then opens Pause. End turn/controller Y is immediate; spending the final Mana automatically settles the enemy turn.
- Floating numbers, bounded movement and sound consume ordered committed events. Enemy attacks present sequentially. Animations never determine outcomes or block input.

## Interaction states and accessibility

Shared tooltips follow Alchemy's immediate hover behavior: compact charcoal panels, 20px corners, 12px padding, content-sized width capped at 120% of the standard creature artwork width (320px at 100% Tooltip size, scaled with Tooltip size), and a 100ms opacity fade on entry and exit, preferring above with an 8px gap. Details stay pointer-transparent, collision-aware and available on intentional keyboard/controller focus. Clicking dismisses details; fresh mouse movement over the artwork restores them. Window blur or a hidden document dismisses open details.

Idle artwork has no visible border; other controls use thin neutral borders. Enabled Buttons scale to 1.035 on hover or intentional navigation focus over 200ms; artwork also animates a 3px gold border on hover. This transient interaction scale preserves fixed layout dimensions and composes with the battle hand's existing lift and rotation. Disabled artwork stays inspectable without scale or border emphasis. Reduced motion removes scale and tooltip motion. Hover brightens controls; focus uses an external dashed muted-gold outline; selection uses a solid external ring. Knockout adds grayscale and dimming. Talent effects and unlock requirements stay visible without hovering. Unavailable talents distinguish missing points from incomplete earlier rows, remain readable and focusable for their tooltip, and cannot issue purchase commands. All non-art controls provide at least 44px hit height. Icons have accessible labels and are paired with values or names. Color alone never conveys availability or selection.

Mouse, W/A/S/D/Tab, and standard-mapped controller actions share the same commands and focus paths. Enter/Space and controller A activate focused controls; B/Escape returns one level. Nested dialogs own their focus and restore a connected, enabled opener; fallback goes to the current screen. Text enlargement, reduced motion, and sound preferences persist independently of game saves. Larger text retains its selected size; reflow and explicit pages keep content and controls reachable without scrolling or clipping. Pause, inspection and navigation do not alter the game revision.

The browser and shadcn/Radix own native Tab order, widget navigation, modal trapping and focus return. The game input adapter supplies controller actions and spatial navigation. Focus outlines, focus rings and focus-only hand lifts appear only during intentional keyboard/controller navigation or activation. Pointer movement, clicks or wheel scrolling remove them completely, including inside portaled dialogs. Automatic focus and Escape/B after pointer use do not enable them. A held stick cannot reclaim navigation after pointer/keyboard use until released. Escape and inspection shortcuts act once per key press. Background windows ignore game input; returning to the window or reconnecting a controller requires releasing held controller controls before they can act.

## Layout, artwork size and spacing

The minimum supported renderer content viewport is **1280×720 CSS pixels**, independent of device pixel ratio. Required review sizes are 1280×720, 1280×800, 1920×1080, 2560×1080 and 3440×1440. Smaller windows are unsupported and are not review targets. Electron minimum outer bounds include the native frame, capped by the available work area; fullscreen and monitor changes reapply the display policy.

No player surface may scroll horizontally or vertically: screens, dialogs, panels, tabs and tooltips all follow this contract. Fit actual content; hiding scrollbars or clipping inaccessible content is not a fix. Long content uses explicit pages with standard shadcn Buttons, accessible Previous/Next names and a page indicator. Filter changes reset to page one. Resizing clamps page bounds and keeps the current item on a visible page where possible. Inspection returns to its opener on its original page.

`tokens.css` owns preferred artwork dimensions: portrait 200×266⅔ and landscape 266⅔×200, with equal area. Battle uses creatures 10% larger and hand cards 10% smaller. Up to 900px viewport height, the shared short side is 180px (90%); up to 760px, it is 160px (80%). Larger displays retain the preferred sizes. Preserve artwork ratios, contain rendering and ArtCard ownership. Reflow before reducing artwork; paginate when the minimum size cannot fit. Never automatically scale typography, control hit areas or the whole interface. Text enlargement remains an explicit player preference. The talents overview fills the available screen width with two rows of seven categories. Its portrait artwork grows without a fixed cap to the largest size that fits both the row width and remaining height, reserving full-size category names, available-point labels and gaps.

Use 24px between screen sections and 12px between related items. Page gutters are 32px horizontally and 24px vertically. Dialogs reserve room for the close control. Screens reserve their actual remaining height after notices and errors. Collection uses one artwork row at compact heights and two at larger heights, with pagination controls outside the gallery. Deck, talent rows, passive tiers, Glossary, owned Boons, Campfire and keyword earnings use bounded pages. Settings keeps native category tabs, including Steam Cloud on desktop, without panel scrolling.

Battle keeps enemy and player rows, target feedback, the seven-card hand, Mana, Swap and End turn reachable simultaneously. Compact heights use a side rail for targeting feedback, with equal space on the opposite side to keep both artwork rows horizontally centered. Effects and Block sit below each frame; thin health bars sit inside its bottom edge. Draw and discard use blank portrait ArtCards at 70% of hand-card width and height, with external labels. Draw sits in the lower left below Mana; discard sits in the lower right below End turn. Auto-play is a Play/Pause icon Button beside the upper-right menu, with an accessible pressed state. The hand remains an overlapping quadratic fan at the bottom, including its authored partial bottom-edge clipping; every visible hand card must have a real selectable hit area and full inspection. This is the sole artwork clipping exception and never creates a scroll area. Screen-reader-only content may retain its accessibility clipping.

The source-policy gate rejects scrolling CSS, utilities and imperative scrolling in player UI. The shared browser layout assertion checks document and nested overflow, viewport bounds, hidden clipping, hit areas and obscured controls, including portaled dialogs and tooltips. Screenshots pass that assertion before capture. Geometry checks are regression protection; rendered review still establishes readability and composition.

## Tokens

`src/ui/tokens.css` owns semantic shadcn theme tokens and combat colors. Use `background`/`foreground` for the canvas, `card`/`card-foreground` for artwork surfaces, `popover`/`popover-foreground` for dialogs/tooltips, `primary`/`primary-foreground` for primary actions, `secondary` and `accent` pairs for quiet controls and hover, `muted`/`muted-foreground` for supporting information, `border`/`input` for boundaries, `ring` for focus, and `destructive` for destructive actions. Tailwind's theme mappings reference these tokens directly. Existing gameplay aliases resolve to these same roles; they are not independent palettes. Damage types, health and block remain separate semantic game tokens.

The color policy rejects raw hexadecimal/function/named colors in player UI, direct Tailwind palette colors (including black/white and arbitrary hex colors), and inline JSX styles. Palette changes belong in the token owner; contrast tests validate foreground/background role pairs. Use opacity/state variants of semantic utilities instead of inventing colors per component.

- Typography: locally bundled Inter Variable, regular rules, medium controls, semibold names, tabular numbers. 16px rules/controls, 20px names, 24px screen headings, 14px secondary information. Inspection enlarges rules to 18px and names to 28px. Font license: `Docs/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
- Spacing: a 4px grid, using layout instead of decorative separators or panels.
- Color: pure black canvas (#000), neutral charcoal surfaces (#141414), ivory text (#ece6db), neutral secondary text (#b3b3b3), neutral gray borders (#484848), and muted gold (#d9b56f). Muted-gold primary actions and selection/focus indicators. Solid selection outline, separate solid focus outline, neutral targetable border. Disabled/unaffordable cards dim; knockout adds grayscale and zero health.
- Geometry: shadcn’s default 0.625rem base radius (10px at 16px text), with semantic sm/md/lg/xl radii derived at 0.6/0.8/1/1.4 times the base. Artwork uses the base radius; standard controls keep their component radius. No sharp visible surface corners or `rounded-none` overrides, thin borders, no decorative shadows, glows or textures (the external selection ring is an interaction indicator). Artwork has no UI scrim.
- Motion: 150ms general transitions, with the shared hover and tooltip timings above; respect reduced motion. Gameplay commits before visual feedback and input never waits on animations.
- Icons: Lucide, with accessible names and immediate hover/focus details where applicable. Status effects show icons only; their counts and names live in tooltips.

The hand shows at most seven playable cards. Extra draws remain entirely hidden in an unplayable buffer, with no count, controls or inspection view. Playing a card fills the open slot from the buffer in draw order. Ending the turn discards the visible hand and retains the buffer.

## Floating combat feedback

Fauna adapts Alchemy's floating feedback to its three-creature rows. Committed events carry damage type, Critical and periodic flags, and structured status/outcome identities. Presentation never parses prose to discover rules. Descriptive text remains in the accessible battle log. Damage, healing, Block and additive status gains use typed icons and signed numbers. Notices use icons without visible prose; Cleanse/Purge pair their icon with each affected effect, immunity pairs Shield with the rejected status, skipped turns pair Timer with the control effect, and Death's Door retains Skull. Existing hand/resource readouts acknowledge draws and Mana; this work adds no Alchemy-only reactions or gameplay.

Within an action, sum actual damage packets by recipient and type, retaining whether any packet was Critical. Keep costs and non-impact losses separate. Notices precede losses, then gains, in stable order. Suppress redundant zero entries when the action has another meaningful result; an otherwise ineffective action retains its effect icon and 0. Identical notices deduplicate. Cross-action additive merges match recipient, effect, type/resource, impact/periodic identity and direction within 250ms of the original burst. Non-additive values never sum across actions. Merges preserve entry/burst IDs, animation, start and expiry. Reserve initial numeric digits plus one extra digit and the sign; overflow becomes a separate entry. Unmatched incoming entries get a new burst and lifetime. Evict before merging when necessary so fresh effects cannot disappear with an old burst.

The numeric base is 37.8 CSS pixels at 100% text, bold, with proportional icons, tabular reserved numbers, dark outlines and static shadows. Values live in `src/ui/tokens.css`. Pop from 0.5× to 2× by 150ms, settle to 1.8× at 200ms, hold through 400ms, then shrink to 1× by 900ms. Rise with an accelerating curve after the hold, up to 240px, and fade over the final 250ms of the 1,100ms lifetime. Critical damage retains its type and size, adding a pale 140ms flash and gold 480ms glow. Further merges do not restart an existing Critical accent.

One noninteractive viewport overlay follows artwork bounds through portrait motion and layout changes, above card flights and below modals. Newest actions use the lower portrait anchor; older actions move upward over 150ms. Reserve space for the full pop. Up to three numeric entries use one centered column; larger bursts use two, with notices spanning both. Keep at most three bursts per creature. Clamp travel and placement to the viewport, offset overlapping creature groups vertically, and fade older groups early when space is exhausted. Evicted groups never reappear. Artwork and numeric text never shrink to fit the viewport. End turn keeps the battlefield in its stable viewport so resolved outcomes remain visible; keyboard/controller command focus retains its existing owner.

Reduced motion removes pop, travel and repositioning, retaining the same lifetime, semantic icons and steady Critical glow. Switching motion back on does not replay existing feedback. Incoming commands never wait for animations; interrupted sequences reveal remaining numbers without replaying their sound or impact. Pause and teardown cancel pending presentation, clear feedback and release timers, animation handles and portrait tracking; resume does not replay actions. Tracking runs only while bursts exist. Final settlement remains immediate.

## Combat movement and sound

Adapt Alchemy's outcome-driven impact/cue selection and bounded recoil rather than copying its artwork or audio assets. Each presented action selects one strongest nonzero cue, with damage winning ties over healing and block. Positive health loss applies a short 180ms portrait recoil of 3px for periodic or small hits, 6px for medium hits, and 10px for large hits; fully absorbed and ineffective hits have no health-loss recoil. Recoil is bounded per creature and disabled with reduced motion. Floating feedback retains its damage type and timing.

Original synthesized cues distinguish damage families, healing, gaining block, and absorption. Audio unlocks on user interaction and is optional; unavailable devices or autoplay denial cannot interrupt play. Desktop autoplay supports controller-only play. Settings offers a persistent Sound on/off setting. Pausing cancels pending presentation without replaying it on resume. Sounds, animation handles, listeners and pending timers are disposed on teardown; visual aggregation never invents extra impacts or sounds.

## Battle interaction decisions

- Enemy details open below their artwork and readout; ally and hand details open above. Radix collision handling may shift/flip to remain on-screen. Selecting an action closes its tooltip while targeting; hovering/focusing the card again restores its rules. While targeting, creature hover/focus prioritizes forecasts; pinned creature inspection remains available.
- Health loss is striped on the bar, with exact typed Health damage beside the target and absorbed damage labeled separately. Block previews show old → projected Block, including depletion to zero. Status forecasts show gains, removals and changed stacks, including effects on creatures other than the chosen recipient. Forecast readouts reserve space and wrap their full outcomes without scrolling or moving the artwork. Lethal previews add a small skull alongside the health bar. Numeric health totals remain in hover/inspection only.
- Invalid targets dim slightly; knocked-out creatures retain their slots with dimmed grayscale artwork and empty health bars. Empty battlefield clicks cancel selection. A/D retains remembered valid targets.
- Selected cards softly fade the Mana crystals they would spend. Actual Mana changes only after acceptance. Unavailable cards stay dimmed and inspectable; activation gives a short shake and quiet denial cue, with a brief visible reason such as Not enough Mana or the acting creature being Stunned/Frozen. Sound and reduced-motion settings apply; rejected input never publishes gameplay or saves.
- Mouse use stays hover-driven: no programmatic battle targeting focus, automatic hand focus, focus-only tooltip or focus lift. Navigation keys, Tab and controller navigation intentionally activate focus. After play, keyboard/controller focus moves to the nearest remaining hand slot. Pointer selection itself still lifts the selected card.

The shared [keyword catalog](KEYWORDS.md) assigns every current and planned keyword a semantic `--keyword-<id>` foreground. Current resource and damage tokens alias that palette. Card prose, passives, glossary definitions, talent tabs and XP feedback consume the same identity. Planned keywords remain outside automatic prose highlighting and the player glossary until their rules are implemented.

## Shared implementation

[Design system](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) maps accepted decisions to shared primitives, tokens and presentation owners, records enforcement and remaining validation. Screen styles own layout; control interaction states belong in the shared shadcn skin.
