# Localized screen review

A task-owned Vite/Chromium preview inspected German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Russian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese at 1280×720, 125% text and reduced motion. The screen matrix covered the main menu, Display/Sound/Battle Settings and language picker, Collection and creature inspection, talent overview and tree, Glossary, battle and pause, draw pile, recruitment and replacement, Boon choice, available and unavailable Merchant, Campfire and journey results. It also inspected a completed battle's reward and journey gains and sampled Boon and Merchant hover details at 125% tooltip size. These are selected game states, not every possible localized item or outcome.

Before/after frames cover the Japanese menu, French and Russian Merchant, French reward, and French, Russian and Japanese talent tree at 1280×720 with 125% text and 1920×1080 with default text. The Japanese Collection label stays on one line; Merchant headings and the Gold wallet no longer overlap; the French keyword and level have separate space; and unavailable talent rules and requirements remain readable. The normal-sized menu retains its previous width. Final captures reported no page or layout errors; the first Vite optimization reload and an early, incorrectly routed talent-tree capture were discarded and repeated.

Separate browser interactions opened Collection with the keyboard and restored focus on return, purchased a Russian Merchant Boon with the expected Gold and inventory changes, and activated an unavailable talent without changing the save. The latter's computed rules and status colors are `rgb(232, 227, 217)` and `rgb(170, 160, 146)` on `rgb(8, 8, 7)` at full card opacity. Raw frames, capture records and interaction results are retained in `reports/localized-screen-review/` with `.retention-keep`.

Captures used the current application source and the browser dependencies shared from main; after capture, dependency setup replaced the task's link with a private install matching the same lockfile, whose only change from the previously shared install was Electron. Browser inspection is not native desktop, physical controller, Steam client or Steam Deck verification, and it does not establish artistic approval.
