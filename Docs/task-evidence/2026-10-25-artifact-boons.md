# Ten additional artwork-led Boons

Implemented Apiary Smoker, Brookskip Disc, Fallingstar Anvil, Pleated Pagoda, Calendula Soap, Nectar Dipper, Saddle Pouch, Dune Shuttle, Story Puzzle Cube and Mineral Bookend with the October 25 approved revisions. Exact player text lives in the [Boon rule owner](../../src/game/rules/boons.ts); [content contracts](../CONTENT.md#additional-artwork-led-boons) record effect semantics and interactions. These enter the ordinary journey reward and Merchant pools without new battle controls.

Puzzle Cube tracks three different successful applications across all enemies, following the owner's explicit clarification. A repeated type does not advance the set. The third different type clears the shared set before Purge reactions and removes one beneficial effect from its recipient. Later applications in the same area ability can begin a new set. Development save format 61 stores the set, rejects duplicate/unknown types and progress without ownership, preserves progress through turns/reload, and clears it for a new battle. Old development saves are rejected without migration. The save contract test now derives the current version from its canonical owner rather than retaining a stale version literal. Historical command/modifier oracles retain their original Boons and hashes and omit only dormant empty progress.

Brookskip Disc retains the original hit and copies the Water damage actually absorbed by Block to a second living enemy through normal defenses. Its finite chain cannot add another critical roll, attack bonuses, buildup, retaliation or another chain. Anvil creates Stun buildup without Stun damage and retains ordinary protection, immunity and control rewards. Apiary Smoker prevents new Nature, Water and Fire Thorns on Blinded enemies without removing existing stacks. Food values use one owner for display, policy assessment and execution; double magnitude preserves Mana cost, Consume, capacity and shared repeats. Actual draw rewards exclude copies, returns, generated cards and buffer promotion. Saddle Pouch only grants its extra incoming-owner draw for an ability swap from backup.
