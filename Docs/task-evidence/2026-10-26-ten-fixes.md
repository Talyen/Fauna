# Ten Auto-play and content fixes

Six reproducible Auto-play defects and four authoring-boundary gaps were fixed from local main `5dfef3b8`.

| #   | Issue                                                                                                                                             | Correction                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | False Horizon was skipped when every enemy had Nullify, although it could spend those wards                                                       | Treat removing eligible wards as useful                                                                |
| 2   | Auto-play omitted legal Echo recipients for Apple and Trail Bread                                                                                 | Assess the living battlefield and shared automatic-healing recipients                                  |
| 3   | Coolfront was skipped against Freeze-damage wards even when it could Cleanse an allied Poison effect                                              | Include useful authored Cleanse effects when assessing attacks                                         |
| 4   | Bloom Cradle and Water Remembers spent Mana when their draw could only return the played card and provided no Healing                             | Require useful draw or support outcomes, retaining Mana healing, draw Boons, copies and Echo responses |
| 5   | Mushroom Guard was skipped at full Health and capped Block even when Clean Slate could remove Poison through supplemental Healing                 | Include supplemental Healing overflow utility                                                          |
| 6   | Ordinary attack assessments ignored deterministic damage modifiers, including enemy Burn resistance, and could mark surviving enemies as defeated | Reuse the combat damage modifier owner without RNG or mutation                                         |
| 7   | Authoring accepted Block retention above 100%, allowing decay to create Block and potentially exceed saved stack bounds                           | Bound the complete tier's combined retention                                                           |
| 8   | Authoring accepted Unfinished Journey allowances above the saved counter's maximum                                                                | Share the counter limit with content validation                                                        |
| 9   | Recipient-only Healing and Cleanse could be attached to primaries that never deliver a friendly-recipient pass                                    | Reject unsupported delivery combinations                                                               |
| 10  | Supplemental damage and target Purge could be attached to primaries without an individual opposing recipient, silently dropping the effects       | Reject unsupported opposing-recipient combinations                                                     |

The current authored catalog remains valid. The last four fixes reject unsupported future content; they do not change current creature kits. Auto-play policy version is 36. Ranking snapshot changes cover removed no-op draws and corrected deterministic damage assessments; combat execution baselines are unchanged.
