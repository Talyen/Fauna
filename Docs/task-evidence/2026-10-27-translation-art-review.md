# Translation and artwork review

Reviewed the 164 Boon rules, the names of the 47 new Boons, all card rules, combat log messages, keyword definitions, and selected navigation, recovery, merchant, replacement and results strings against their English sources in German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Russian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese. This is a focused linguistic review, not a native-speaker qualification of every catalog entry. The complete catalogs also pass the repository's source, placeholder and glyph checks.

The review corrected the Turn and Dodge descriptions to match their gameplay conditions, plus the German Snowbell name, Japanese Overflow Cup rule and Simplified Chinese Chamomile Compress rule. The focused Quiet Pinwheel regression confirms controlled allies can Dodge with that Boon.

Reviewed all 47 new Boon illustrations and the four Ravelland illustrations at source scale and in rendered views. Task-owned Vite and Chromium captures used a fresh context at 1280×720, 125% text and reduced motion. Nineteen Boon-selection captures cover the new Boon set; Ravelland was also inspected in collection and in retained battle evidence. The rendered Boon art remains recognizable in its frame and the localized corrected Boon tooltips and Turn and Dodge glossary entries fit in the captured views. The captures recorded no page or layout errors. Raw frames and capture scripts are retained in `reports/translation-art-review/` with `.retention-keep`.

This is focused browser evidence; it does not establish native desktop, Windows packaging, physical controller, Steam client or Steam Deck verification, nor final artistic approval.
