# Screen specifications

Read the relevant section for the changed flow. [Shared UI rules](../UI.md) own accessibility, tokens, layout and the proportional review procedure; [menu design](../MENU_DESIGN.md) and [journey design](../EXPEDITION_DESIGN.md) own navigation and progression. Implementation lives in the [menu](../../src/ui/menu/), [inspection](../../src/ui/inspection/), [Collection](../../src/ui/collection/) and [Settings](../../src/ui/settings/) owners.

## Other screens

Screen headings place Back in the leading slot and Open menu in the trailing slot of a balanced frame up to 640px wide, with the title centered independently and 24px section gaps. Journey screens also offer Deck beside Back with a 12px related-control gap; equal side reservations keep the title centered. Header buttons and battle corner controls use compact 32px visible surfaces and 16px icons within 44px hit areas. Back returns one navigation level, including from talent trees to the overview and from visits to the encounter; it never undoes completed gameplay. The root main menu retains its simple title.

### Starter draft

First-journey starter selection shows three offers from the configured onboarding pool and commits one creature. Later journeys show three offers from Collection for each of three draft rounds, unless a Legendary is chosen first and completes the draft alone; later rounds exclude Legendaries. The Recruit button beneath an offer commits the pick and advances to the next offer. Recruit buttons are only as wide as their labels and padding. The final required Recruit immediately starts the journey, with no Begin journey button; resuming a completed draft also starts it automatically. Artwork opens inspection without committing a pick; Back returns to the same offer. Inspecting, canceling and reloading preserve choices and offers. Artwork uses the bounded shared sizing profiles; controls retain their hit areas.

### Main menu and Talents

Main menu has narrower buttons, with a centered gold default Button for Continue/Play above a two-by-two grid of ghost Buttons for Talents, Collection, Glossary and Settings. Conditional choices add rows; launching never automatically resumes gameplay. Pause is a compact vertical shadcn Popover anchored to the Open menu button, without a dimmed backdrop or visible title. Talents is full screen with a centered heading, an icon-only Open menu hamburger button to the right of the heading, and a centered keyword artwork overview with at most five categories per row opening dedicated trees. Category names use 16px labels and available-point counts use 13px labels beneath the artwork, both respecting text-size preferences. Fixed caption line boxes preserve artwork sizing and layout when labels grow. When no category has available points, empty point labels reserve no space. Each tree shows available points, exact XP and its Progress bar, followed by compact cards with icon beside name, full effect and visible availability requirements. Trees include all applicable Alchemy talents for shared categories, in rows of two with the final row allowed a single talent. The hamburger opens the originating main or pause menu directly, including from a tree. Escape/controller B returns from a tree to the overview, then to the originating menu. Purchases are immediate. The tree header names the category-specific reset, which immediately refunds that category and is disabled when nothing is allocated. The artwork overview replaces category tabs; the authored catalog owns talent definitions.

### Creature and action inspection

Outside Collection, ordinary creature hover/focus details contain only name, Block, current effects, and passive; no action lists. Starter hover/focus adds the kit keywords and three action names with their current Mana costs, without committing selection. Outside battle and Collection, creature inspection is a symmetrical artwork-first composition on an opaque warm near-black backdrop. The creature name is the centered main heading, followed by 4:3 creature artwork and three equal 3:4 ability thumbnails. Inspection uses the viewport-fitted `--inspection-art-width` from `src/ui/inspection/inspection.css`, capped by `--inspection-art-max-width` (480px); creature width multiplies that budget by `--inspection-creature-scale` (0.8), and the three-ability row uses `--inspection-ability-scale` (1.2) after accounting for gaps. The wider ability row and smaller creature artwork share a center axis, with 12px between abilities. Native aspect ratios, selected typography and control hit areas stay unchanged. Beneath the art, the current named trait, five progress stars and current passive description remain visible. Stars fill from left to right with permanent rank; all five are empty at zero stars. They are progress indicators, with no rank controls or separate rank view in inspection. Collection retains its full rank progression tab. Ability names and rules appear only on hover or keyboard/controller focus. Inspection never contains a Recruit or Choose action and never commits gameplay. Dialog titles remain available to assistive technology. Battle enemy hover/focus details show current combat effects; full species abilities belong in Collection. Intents stay hidden.
Outside battle and Collection, opening a thumbnail pins its artwork in the same shadcn Dialog, with final effects still on hover/focus. A/D and controller left/right cycle through the species' three actions without Previous/Next buttons. Back/Escape/controller B returns to the creature and restores focus to the current action thumbnail. Every creature and action inspection has a visible icon-only Back button with an accessible name. A subsequent Back closes inspection and restores the original opener. X/outside-click closes the whole dialog using standard Dialog behavior. Direct hand/deck action inspection cycles the same kit and closes on Back; it does not introduce a creature parent view. Card rules show final effects with no bonus breakdown. Definitions stay exclusively in the menu Glossary.

### Collection

Collection orders collected species first and offers All, Collected and Uncollected filters. Only recruited species, including the committed onboarding starter, count as collected; encountering or victory-unlocking a species does not collect it. Collected artwork shows five external star icons filled to its permanent rank, with names and creature details on hover or keyboard focus. Locked artwork is grayscale and noninteractive, with no tooltip or unlock requirement. Collected creatures open a full Collection page with the current named trait and all three base abilities readable at once: portrait artwork, name, Mana cost and full rules. These species rules are independent of journey upgrades, talents and temporary battle modifiers; hand hover/focus retains final battle rules. Rank progression uses a native tab on the same page with paginated tiers, without nested overlays. Back/Escape/controller B returns directly to the same gallery filter, page and creature focus. Open menu returns to the originating menu. Collection remains available from battle Pause; returning preserves committed combat and leaves Auto-play off.

### Victory and results

Encounter victories first show a centered Victory step with earned keyword progress and a separate Gold row beneath it; keyword icons and level labels match their keyword colors, with compact progress bars. Continue opens a separate choice step headed Choose a Recruit, with no earnings repeated beside the choices. This presentation step never grants rewards or changes the committed game state; resuming an unchosen recruitment shows the earnings again before the choice, without granting them twice; a banked full-roster recruit resumes directly at team confirmation. Boon choices open directly after recruitment without another earnings step. Ordinary combat shows Choose a Recruit with the defeated enemies (one in the first battle, two in the second, three thereafter), including owned and repeated species; intermediate Bosses offer Legendary recruitment followed by portrait Boon choices. Recruit choices show a small New! chip for a first Collection unlock, or five stars with earned ranks filled and the incoming star outlined with a plus marker; maximum rank shows five filled stars without a caption. Recruitment choices use a compact content group without Skip; unused pagination space is omitted. Boon choices retain their reserved choice area. Recruit buttons sit below creature artwork; activating artwork opens inspection, and names and rules stay in hover/focus details. Recruit commits Collection progress once. With room in the roster it joins immediately; at five creatures it opens Choose your team, with no Back or Cancel. The centered exchange artwork and opposing-arrow icon sit above one quiet Team tray showing all five current individuals without names, Health or pagination. Artwork activation immediately previews one exchange with the shared battle arc animation, preserving input during motion; activating the selected member or upper artwork reverses it. Names and rules remain available through hover/focus and inspection. The single centered Confirm action commits the displayed roster or keeps the original roster if unchanged. Previews never save, consume RNG or grant progress; a saved recruit resumes with the original roster preview. Selecting/skipping a Boon advances without Continue. An empty Boon pool skips that choice; the final Boss offers recruitment before results and defeat goes directly to results. Journey results show only the outcome and permanent gains with keyword names, levels and progress bars, without numeric XP gains or fractions; available talent points belong on Talents. Omit teams, creature unlocks, journey statistics and temporary boons. Results appear only when the journey ends and cannot be reopened from the main menu. Boons use their dedicated portrait artwork in 3:4 ArtCards with centered external names and hover/focus effects. Development saves do not retain older reward flows.

### Encounter and replacement

Ordinary combat shows one blank landscape placeholder ArtCard labeled Combat. Boss entrances show the named boss with its species artwork and base-trait hover/focus inspection. Boss details reveal no turn intents. Activating either entrance starts battle directly, without a preview dialog. During battle, creatures and actions retain hover/focus details but never open pinned inspection. Draw/discard pile and knockout replacement dialogs retain hover/focus details without nested inspection. The encounter screen keeps Deck available. Free Swap and Lineup controls are removed. When a deployed creature is knocked out and living backup remain, a centered shadcn Dialog asks “Choose a replacement”, showing that creature above the living backup at shared artwork sizes. Choosing a backup commits immediately; successive knockouts receive successive choices. Escape and outside clicks do not dismiss this required choice. Focus starts on the first living backup and returns to the hand or End turn after the final choice. Backup remain hidden on the battlefield. The prompt is reconstructed from committed roster state after reload. Both abilities and knockout replacements persist the changed formation for later battles.

### Settings and Glossary navigation

Settings and Glossary are full screens with centered content and icon-only Open menu hamburger buttons to the right of their headers; Glossary uses one named, focusable vertical reading region with wheel, keyboard and controller scrolling. The hamburger and Escape/controller B restore the originating main or pause menu choice. Child menu dialogs temporarily replace Pause so only their focus trap is active. End journey ends immediately from the hamburger pause menu; ordinary rewards and End turn also have no confirmation.

## Collection and audio settings

Collection has All, Collected and Uncollected shadcn tabs. Collected creatures reuse the artwork-only grid and hover/focus details, then open the full creature page on activation; only collected species enter later starter drafts. Uncollected creatures remain locked and reveal no kit details. Filters and page anchors are transient; returning from a creature page restores its gallery entry. Browsing never grants discovery.

Settings uses Alchemy-style category tabs (Display, Sound and Battle), icon labels aligned with controls, and body-size pale-gold section headings on a centered full screen. Display offers 75%, 100% and 125% text-size radios. Sound contains a Sound switch, Master/Music/Sound effects sliders in 10-point steps with percentage readouts, and Mute in background. Sliders retain native keyboard behavior; controller and W/A/S/D navigation adjusts them horizontally and moves between rows vertically. Rows wrap controls beneath labels when space is limited; text and control sizes stay fixed. Audio preferences persist separately from progression; setting changes apply live without replaying actions. Both size controls default to 100%; every audio slider defaults to 50%. Tabs are centered with real visible borders on every tab: neutral inactive borders and gold selected/hover borders, without a background strip. Use the standard 12px gap between tabs, 12px inner horizontal padding and regular light active text on the warm near-black canvas; keyboard focus retains its outline and a visible neutral border. Use distinct Lucide icons within a screen. The Settings title is 24px semibold light text; section headings use body-size pale gold text with fading rules; option labels are 16px light text, with muted supporting text (all respect the selected text size). Diagnostic reports and their tab/copy action are removed. Rendered review and a listening pass remain required for these flows; DOM and runtime tests alone do not establish acceptance.

## Piles, effects and Campfire

Draw Pile and Discard Pile use the copied Alchemy pile artwork in shared ArtCards beside the centered hand, with accessible inspection names. Each opens the existing Deck-style dialog, including empty state and hover/focus details without nested action inspection. Cards sort independently of draw order. Escape closes the pile dialog and returns focus to its connected opener. Pile browsing never commits commands.

On the battlefield, combat effects remain visible outside creature artwork, including defenses, periodic stacks, remaining control windows, immunity, Death's Door grace and pending talent rewards. Status notices also enter committed feedback and the accessible battle log; sound is supplementary. Campfire pages through the whole roster with current Health and the exact capped recovery before its single Rest action. Successful Rest returns immediately to the same combat offers and briefly shows committed before/after Health meters and actual recovery for healed creatures, including backup. Navigation and battle remain immediately available. Reduced motion settles the meters directly; cleanup cancels animation and expiry work. Campfire remains visible while any roster creature is missing at least 30% maximum Health.

Tooltip size has its own 75% / 100% / 125% Settings choice. It changes tooltip type and width independently of ordinary text size; it never scales artwork. Both preferences persist outside progression. Owned Boons use the shared portrait ArtCard and native modal focus behavior.

## Support visits and platform settings

Boon Merchant opens directly from its existing 4:3 location ArtCard, and appears only when at least one stocked item is affordable. Its current source location is an explicitly reused placeholder. Use a centered three-item shop shelf: prepared portrait Boons with no labels below the artwork, a Gold wallet in the heading row and the centered italic line “Limit 1 per customer - No Refunds!” above the offers, aligned secondary purchase Buttons and a centered Leave control. Boon names and rules stay in hover/focus inspection. The wallet and purchase controls use the shared gold-colored Coins icon. Artwork remains inspectable on hover/focus, with rules and journey-only duration in the shared tooltip. Unaffordable purchase controls remain focusable, explain Not enough Gold, and cannot issue commands. Buying replaces the shelf with a reward screen showing the purchased Boon, name, rules, For this journey and Continue; keyboard/controller focus moves to Continue. The grant and spending are already committed, including after reload. Leave, Back and reward Continue complete the Merchant room and prepare fresh destinations rather than restoring the previous offers; another Merchant is excluded. Menus preserve the active shelf or reward. Artwork uses the shared sizing profiles and text retains the selected size. Campfire previews exact capped roster recovery.

Desktop Display settings add Windowed and Fullscreen radios, reflecting confirmed native state. Browser omits the control. Steam Cloud reconciliation and standalone-progress adoption run automatically without a Settings surface, save choices, status messages or retry controls. Competing saves are preserved before automatic resolution; progression fields are never merged. Startup automatically continues locally after failure or its bounded wait, following the [recovery policy](../UI.md#recovery-and-persistent-failures).

Steam overlay activation pauses through the existing menu, preserves higher-priority inspection, clears targeting and stops Auto-play. Held confirmation cannot activate another action on return; the player resumes deliberately. Fullscreen transitions, support visits and cloud recovery require rendered review at the existing viewports and text sizes; native/Steam acceptance remains separate.

Settings uses a compact 640px maximum content width and keeps a centered, stable screen frame and tab-row position when switching categories. Mounted inactive panels reserve the tallest category’s natural height rather than an extra fixed minimum. Tab labels retain regular weight in every state so button widths and icons do not shift. Each category fits its panel when practical; long secondary content may use a named, focusable vertical scroll region while tabs and Back remain reachable. Panel content switches instantly without fading, overlap or movement. Tab border and text colors retain their subtle 150ms transition; reduced motion removes those transitions. Native shadcn/Radix tab selection, visibility and focus behavior remain unchanged.

Selected tabs retain their appearance on hover. Across selected elements, pointer hover never thickens the border or selection ring. Preserve the visible keyboard/controller focus indicator independently of selection.

## Adaptive browsing and effect borders

Campfire measures available width before choosing how many creatures fit on one row. Recruitment team confirmation shows its bounded five-member roster on one row at supported viewports. Talent trees measure the available height after their heading, progress and pagination to show up to three authored rows. Resizing preserves the first visible item and keeps every item reachable through pagination. PagedItems reserves measured row heights on partial pages and the pagination control height even on a single page, keeping headers and actions stable and preventing capacity from oscillating when controls appear. Artwork sizes, text preferences and control hit areas remain unchanged.

Settings uses body-size pale-gold section headings with fading rules, separating section labels from page titles. Labels and controls share aligned columns, with values aligned to the control edge. Native tabs retain their reserved tallest-panel layout.

Action and Boon artwork derives animated shine borders from every distinct keyword in its displayed effect text, including aliases; keyword-less effects use neutral Physical. Creature artwork reveals its kit palette on hover or intentional focus, with active-actor and Death's Door treatments in battle. The [shared effects owner](../UI.md#keyword-visual-effects) also supplies contextual plasma, rising particles and short-label text shines. Names and rules remain in ordinary inspection. Keyboard focus retains its separate outer outline, and selected actions and battle targeting retain their existing indicators. Unavailable actions do not gain availability shine; legitimate inspection still supplies its palette.

Legendary combatants occupy the center battlefield position; their Minions occupy the side positions. Both rows reserve three positions and share the fitted artwork size. The hand uses a six-degree fan step with a five-pixel quadratic drop; its 3:4 contain-rendered artwork remains unchanged. The required replacement dialog shows the knocked-out creature above living backup, with an up/down icon separating the rows; only backup choices can commit a replacement.
